Socrates

Biography

Socrates (469–399 BCE) lived in Athens and is regarded as the father of Western philosophy. He never wrote down his teachings, but his ideas survive through the works of his students, especially Plato. His life ended with a trial and execution, which became a symbol of intellectual freedom and the pursuit of truth.

Main Ideas

  • The Socratic Method: questioning to expose contradictions and seek truth.
  • “The unexamined life is not worth living.”
  • Virtue is knowledge — to know the good is to do the good.

Legacy

Socrates influenced generations of philosophers, especially Plato and Aristotle. His emphasis on dialogue and ethics shaped the foundations of Western thought and continues to inspire critical inquiry today.
