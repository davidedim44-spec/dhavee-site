Contemporary Philosophy

Contemporary philosophy spans the 19th and 20th centuries, exploring existentialism, phenomenology, postmodernism, and political theory. These thinkers challenged traditional metaphysics and emphasized human freedom, power, and meaning.

Key Thinkers

  • Friedrich Nietzsche — “God is dead” and the will to power.
  • Jean‑Paul Sartre — Existentialism and radical freedom.
  • Michel Foucault — Power, knowledge, and social institutions.
  • Jacques Derrida — Deconstruction and language.
  • Hannah Arendt — Political theory and the human condition.

Main Themes

Existence, freedom, power, language, identity, and the structures of society.
