Ancient Philosophy

Ancient philosophy refers to the foundational ideas developed in Greece and Rome between the 6th century BCE and the 6th century CE. It explores questions of reality, knowledge, ethics, and politics.

Key Thinkers

  • Socrates — known for his method of questioning and pursuit of virtue.
  • Plato— developed the Theory of Forms and founded the Academy.
  • Aristotle— emphasized logic, ethics, and natural sciences.

Main Themes

Truth, virtue, justice, metaphysics, and the search for wisdom were central to Ancient Philosophy.
