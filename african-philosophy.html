African Philosophy

African philosophy explores indigenous traditions, cultural identity, and modern intellectual thought across the continent. It addresses themes of community, spirituality, colonialism, liberation, and the search for authentic African perspectives in philosophy.

Key Thinkers

  • Kwame Nkrumah — Political philosophy and Pan‑Africanism.
  • John Mbiti — African religion and philosophy.
  • Sophie Oluwole — Yoruba philosophy and Orunmila tradition.
  • Paulin Hountondji — Critique of ethnophilosophy.

Main Themes

Community, identity, liberation, tradition, spirituality, and the dialogue between African and global philosophy.
