Modern Philosophy

Modern philosophy developed between the 17th and 19th centuries, focusing on reason, science, and human understanding. It marked a shift from medieval scholasticism to new methods of inquiry and critical thought.

Key Thinkers

  • René Descartes — “I think, therefore I am.”
  • Immanuel Kant — Ethics and the limits of reason.
  • David Hume — Empiricism and skepticism.
  • Baruch Spinoza — Rationalism and ethics.

Main Themes

Reason, knowledge, skepticism, ethics, political philosophy, and the relationship between science and metaphysics.
