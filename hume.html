David Hume

Biography

David Hume (1711–1776) was a Scottish philosopher, historian, and essayist. He is best known as a leading figure of the Enlightenment and a major proponent of empiricism and skepticism. His works challenged traditional views of causation, religion, and human understanding.

Main Ideas

  • Empiricism: All knowledge derives from sensory experience, not innate ideas.
  • Skepticism: Questioned certainty in knowledge, especially regarding causation and metaphysics.
  • Causation: We never perceive causation directly, only constant conjunction
