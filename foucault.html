Michel Foucault

Biography

Michel Foucault (1926–1984) was a French philosopher and social theorist. He studied institutions such as prisons, hospitals, and schools to understand how power and knowledge shape human behavior. His work challenged traditional ideas of authority and truth in modern society.

Main Ideas

  • Power and Knowledge: Power is not just held by rulers but embedded in everyday practices and institutions.
  • Discipline: Modern societies control individuals through surveillance and normalization.
  • Genealogy: Historical analysis of how concepts like madness, sexuality, and punishment evolved.
  • Biopolitics: Governments regulate populations through health, sexuality, and social norms.

Legacy

Foucault’s ideas influenced sociology, political science, cultural studies, and philosophy. His analysis of power and institutions continues to shape debates on social justice, identity, and governance in the contemporary world.
